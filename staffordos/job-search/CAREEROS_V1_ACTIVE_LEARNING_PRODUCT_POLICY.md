# CareerOS V1 Active Learning Product Policy

Questions are selected per customer, not copied from Ross. The policy ranks unresolved capability dimensions by:

`expected reusable requirements affected × uncertainty reduction × evidence availability × specialist/scope sensitivity`

Labels, desired ranking movement, interest, self-confidence, workflow, and application outcomes are excluded from capability-question selection.

Questions must name the capability and scope being resolved, offer neutral states, preserve `DIRECT`, `TRANSFERABLE`, `PARTIAL`, `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`, and `KEEP_UNRESOLVED`, and record graph/taxonomy versions. A decision can affect many requirement concepts only through deterministic, provenance-preserving projection.

Product metric:

`safe exact requirements informed / customer decisions required`

A3's 160.4 is a single-profile diagnostic, not a promise. Beta reporting must show median, range, and specialist/scope error rates across customers.
