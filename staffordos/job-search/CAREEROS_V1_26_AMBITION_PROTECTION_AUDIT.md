# CareerOS V1.26 Ambition Protection Audit

## Findings

The authority contracts protect against several known errors, but the repository-visible Career Evidence corpus is too thin to guarantee fair evaluation of ambitious opportunities.

| Risk | Current protection | Remaining risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title gap becomes capability gap | Title is not an allowed evidence source; V1.24 seniority projection supports upward stretch | Missing employment scope can still leave roles unresolved |
| Industry/domain gap becomes capability gap | Domain is separate and transferable | Specialist evidence is sparse |
| Tool mismatch becomes capability gap | Technology facts require evidence and limitations | Private tool evidence is not inspectable in this repo |
| Missing evidence becomes inability | Mapping states distinguish MISSING and UNKNOWN | A low raw coverage score can still suppress viable roles |
| Seniority stretch becomes failure | `UPWARD_STRETCH_WITH_SUPPORTED_SCOPE` exists | Scope/leadership evidence is incomplete |
| Low self-confidence lowers fit | Tests and contracts exclude it | Must remain excluded in future model work |
| Interest/workflow becomes fit | Evaluation contracts explicitly separate them | No new risk found |

## Mechanisms capable of under-selling Ross

1. Core responsibility evidence is private and not available as a stable, inspectable authority snapshot to every offline evaluation.
2. Employment title/date/scope conflicts prevent direct classification even when transferable responsibility mappings exist.
3. Generic repository/project artifacts are not sufficient to prove professional or customer use.
4. Missing technical-depth evidence causes `UNKNOWN` or `EVIDENCE_GAP`, which is correct authority semantics but can lower experimental scores.
5. Existing mappings expose transferable support without enough bounded scope, outcome, or recency detail.

## No prohibited inference found

No evidence was found that self-confidence, interest, workflow state, title identity, or aspiration is being used as Career Evidence in the canonical contracts.
