# Private Beta Privacy Contract

CareerOS stores customer profile fields, user-provided pasted career text, source-backed candidate facts, customer review decisions, confirmed CareerFacts, onboarding state, and tenant-scoped audit metadata to provide the product. The data is customer-private and is not shared across tenants.

Customers can export and delete their CareerOS-owned records. Passwords, session secrets, operator artifacts, and research/evaluation data are excluded from exports. Binary uploads, automated profile scraping, matching, capability onboarding, and job discovery are outside this beta surface. Public privacy/legal pages and retention terms remain required before invitations are issued.
