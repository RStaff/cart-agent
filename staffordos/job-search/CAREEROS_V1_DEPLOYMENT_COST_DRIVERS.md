# CareerOS Deployment Cost Drivers

Recommended classification: `LOW_BETA_COST` relative to a managed Kubernetes stack, subject to provider approval and actual plan selection.

Cost drivers are application compute, managed PostgreSQL, backups/storage, network egress, logs, domain/TLS, and any rate-limit/database capacity. No dollar estimates are asserted. The selected plan must be reviewed for scale-to-zero behavior, backup inclusion, connection limits, and restore charges before approval.
