# CareerOS V1 Product Boundary

## Product promise

CareerOS builds a reusable, evidence-grounded model of a person's capabilities and evaluates user-provided opportunities against that model. It explains direct support, transferable support, partial support, unknowns, missing evidence, and specialist boundaries.

## V1 does

- accept customer-provided career information and job opportunities;
- derive versioned CareerFacts and evidence links;
- derive a canonical capability profile;
- ask a small, customer-specific set of high-information capability questions;
- evaluate opportunities with the frozen V2D-compatible relationship states;
- explain strong matches, transferable strengths, partial support, evidence gaps, specialist blocks, and unknowns;
- retain profile and opportunity history for return visits.

## V1 does not

- guarantee employment or infer global lack of ability from missing evidence;
- treat titles, companies, domains, interest, workflow, or self-confidence as capability truth;
- submit applications, perform outreach, or act as a recruiter;
- require universal job discovery for the core value proposition;
- expose StaffordOS operator controls or private governance artifacts;
- change the validated V2D model during product onboarding.

Capability Fit, Evidence Confidence, and Pursuit Priority remain separate product concepts.
