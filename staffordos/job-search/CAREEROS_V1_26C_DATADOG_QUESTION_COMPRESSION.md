# CareerOS V1.26C Datadog Question Compression

The Datadog TPM control already has supported or transferable program-delivery evidence. The minimum bounded question set is four:

1. Program accountability: planning, dependencies, risk, and delivery.
2. Leadership scope: direct people management versus influence-led leadership.
3. Technical depth: direct design/implementation versus coordination of technical work.
4. Domain context: technical-solutions operating context versus transferable program capability.

No question is needed for already-supported responsibility evidence. These questions do not alter Datadog score, rank, eligibility, qualification, or recommendation. An answer remains an operator adjudication candidate until separately projected through the canonical CareerEvidence authority.
