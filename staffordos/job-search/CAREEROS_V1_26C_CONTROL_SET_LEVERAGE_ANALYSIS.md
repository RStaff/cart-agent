# CareerOS V1.26C Control-Set Leverage Analysis

The locked 80-role evaluation remains measurement-only. The proposed questions are useful only when they separate supportable ambitious roles from specialist or unsupported controls.

Highest leverage areas are program accountability, cross-functional responsibility, technical implementation depth, AI/automation ownership, and people-management scope. They can explain why a director or adjacent-domain role is supportable without turning generic strategy, operations, finance, payroll, tax, legal, AV, software-engineering, or data-science language into evidence.

No calibration labels, scores, ranks, or V2D inputs changed. A question is rejected as propagation input when its candidates do not share a safe semantic family or when the conflict is inference/source, temporal, other, or insufficient provenance.
