## Discrimination result

Current CareerEvidence can distinguish some responsibility transfer from specialist requirements, but the repository does not expose enough real, structured, provenance-backed Ross evidence to reliably separate:

- ambitious but supportable roles;
- adjacent roles needing better evidence;
- genuine specialist capability gaps.

The principal blocker is evidence authority completeness, not a justified new weight set.
