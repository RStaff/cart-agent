# CareerOS V1.26A Next Stage Decision

## Decision

`PRIVATE_AUTHORITY_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTS_EVIDENCE_EXPANSION`

## Rationale

The private loader finds a large existing fact corpus and a smaller evidence corpus. Existing authority can support a bounded projection for verified credential-specific facts and carefully reviewed transferable responsibilities. However, most facts are conflicting or unknown-support, and the repository cannot safely project them automatically.

## Next mission

Build a read-only, sanitized private-authority projection preview that reports eligible aggregate categories and candidate counts without writing canonical CareerEvidence. Then obtain operator review for employment scope, technical depth, leadership scope, and project outcomes before any promotion.
