# CareerOS V1.26A Control-Set Authority Analysis

The locked calibration/holdout labels remain evaluation authority only. They do not create candidate evidence.

## Positive controls

Strong/good and transferable/stretch controls generally show transferable or partial mappings, especially for program, transformation, AI-adjacent, business-systems, and MarTech responsibilities. Their lower-ranked results are consistent with missing scope, technical-depth, domain, or provenance projection.

## Negative controls

Poor-match/hard-no finance, payroll, tax, AV, software-engineering, and data-science roles remain specialist controls. Generic private leadership/product/technology material must not project as specialist evidence.

## Diagnostic conclusion

Existing private authority is present across both positive and negative lanes, but its high conflict/unknown-support rate means a broad projection could reduce discrimination if it is not specialist-aware and scope-aware. The correct next step is a gated projection audit, not a score change.
