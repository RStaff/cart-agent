# S010.02A Career Evidence Discovery and Canonical Authority

## Mission

`S010_02A_CAREER_EVIDENCE_DISCOVERY_AND_CANONICAL_AUTHORITY` is a read-only
architecture discovery mission for Professional Job Search.

This mission establishes the canonical direction for Career Evidence Authority.
It does not generate resumes, score role fit, create routes, modify runtime
code, add persistence, call AI, invoke Ollama, or change S009.

The required authority chain is:

`FACT -> EVIDENCE -> POSITIONING`

Resumes, applications, interview notes, portfolio copy, and AI recommendations
must be downstream presentation or positioning. They must not become the primary
source of career truth.

## Canonical Authority Model

Minimum authority layers:

1. `CareerEvidenceSource`
   - Captures where a fact came from.
   - Examples: resume file, diploma, certificate, repository artifact, portfolio page, operator-entered fact, employer document, published article, presentation deck.
   - Source records preserve origin, privacy, freshness, exact reference, digest or future integrity reference, limitations, and review status.

2. `CareerFact`
   - Canonical normalized fact.
   - It can represent employment, education, certification, skill, technology, project, product, architecture, presentation, achievement, leadership example, interview story, publication, reference, or resume fact.
   - It must point to one or more evidence sources.

3. `CareerEvidenceLink`
   - Connects a fact to source evidence.
   - It preserves source excerpts, support strength, limitation, and whether the support is direct, partial, transferable, or insufficient.

4. `Positioning`
   - Resume bullet, application answer, portfolio copy, interview narrative, or AI recommendation.
   - It is not primary authority.
   - It must cite approved `CareerFact` IDs and disclose any uncertainty.

5. `Review`
   - Operator or governed review state that promotes, rejects, corrects, supersedes, or limits a fact.
   - Ross remains approval authority.

Authority rule:

`Positioning` may change wording. It may not change facts.

## Entity Authority Requirements

| Entity | Purpose | Minimum authority requirement | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Employment` | Record employer, role, dates, scope, and source | Verified source or Ross-confirmed fact with limitation | Invent titles, dates, employer names, or outcomes |
| `Education` | Record institution, program, degree, dates, and status | Diploma, transcript, resume source, or Ross confirmation | Convert coursework into degree completion without source |
| `Certification` | Record credential, issuer, date, expiration, and identifier if safe | Certificate, issuer record, or Ross confirmation | Claim active status without freshness |
| `Skill` | Record skill name and evidence relationship | Must cite project, employment, education, or certification evidence | Infer proficiency or years from keywords |
| `Technology` | Record technology/tool/platform used | Must cite work artifact, project, employment, or certification source | Treat package presence as personal expertise by itself |
| `Project` | Record bounded work effort and role | Must cite repository, artifact, proof, or operator-confirmed source | Claim client results or commercial outcomes without proof |
| `Product` | Record product/platform context | Must cite product definition or runtime authority | Treat product existence as personal achievement |
| `Architecture` | Record architecture/system design example | Must cite architecture artifact and role boundary | Claim production impact unless proof exists |
| `Presentation` | Record talk, deck, demo, or stakeholder presentation | Must cite deck, invite, recording, or operator confirmation | Claim audience, employer, or publication status without source |
| `Achievement` | Record outcome or accomplishment | Must cite proof and exact observed outcome | Inflate metrics, revenue, customer impact, or scope |
| `LeadershipExample` | Record situation, role, decisions, and outcome | Must cite project/employment evidence or operator confirmation | Convert solo work into people-management claim without source |
| `InterviewStory` | Record STAR-style story derived from facts | Must cite underlying facts and limitations | Add facts for narrative strength |
| `Publication` | Record published work | Must cite URL, document, publisher, or operator confirmation | Treat drafts as published |
| `Reference` | Record reference availability and constraints | Must be operator-confirmed and private | Expose contact details to models or applications without approval |
| `ResumeFact` | Record fact approved for resume use | Must cite approved facts and evidence links | Let resume wording become primary authority |

## Resume Authority Boundary

No canonical resume authority exists today.

Future resume handling rules:

- A resume file is an artifact, not primary truth.
- A resume bullet is positioning.
- A resume fact must cite canonical `CareerFact` and `CareerEvidenceLink` records.
- Tailoring may adjust emphasis, ordering, and wording.
- Tailoring may not add unverified claims, unsupported metrics, unsupported years, inflated titles, or fabricated tools.
- Every submitted resume version must preserve exact version identity and source fact references.
- Older resumes may be historical evidence, but they must not automatically become verified fact.
- Conflicting resume versions must remain visible until Ross resolves them.

## Failure Closed Rules

Future validators and adapters must fail closed when:

- a fact has no source
- a source belongs to the wrong workspace
- a resume fact has no approved underlying fact
- a skill has no evidence
- years of experience are inferred
- a credential lacks issuer or review authority
- an achievement lacks proof
- a publication is only a draft
- a reference exposes contact details without approval

- conflicting sources are silently resolved
