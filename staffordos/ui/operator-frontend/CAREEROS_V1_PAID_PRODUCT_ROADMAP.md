# CareerOS Paid Product Roadmap

| Capability | Private beta must-have | Private beta validation | Paid V1 | Post-V1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multiple experiences and living profile | Yes | Measure completion and repeat additions | Yes | Growth history |
| Conversational career discovery | Contract and bounded draft | Validate question burden and usefulness | Yes, after runtime authorization | Richer coaching |
| Multi-job workspace and comparison | One saved job path | Validate independent opportunities | Yes | Saved searches |
| Explainable match and evidence gaps | Yes | Observe comprehension | Yes | Application workflows |
| Resume/document upload | No | Validate demand only | Only after storage/security proof | Advanced document intelligence |
| Resume tailoring and interview stories | No | Capture requests | Likely paid V1 | Specialized coaching |
| Job-source adapters and discovery | Paste only | Validate source demand | Selective paid V1 | Broader providers |
| Exportable career intelligence | Existing export | Validate usefulness | Yes | Portfolio/history products |

The matching architecture remains shared across these surfaces. Provider-specific ingestion must stop at the opportunity/requirement contract and must not alter matching semantics.
