# CareerOS Private Beta Definition Of Done

## Account
Signup, login, logout, and session persistence work through invite-only customer routes.

## Career Foundation
Customers can add multiple experiences, review and correct proposed facts, revisit the profile, continue later, and add new information without replacing prior authority. Current facts reviewed is distinct from the reversible “complete for now” story status.

## Career Story
Paste/type works through bounded provenance-safe intake. Talk creates a bounded reviewable text draft. Document upload remains explicitly disabled until binary storage controls are proven.

## Capabilities And Jobs
Capability review is bounded and provenance-linked. Multiple opportunities can coexist and be analyzed independently. Job input never contaminates career authority.

## Match And Customer Value
DIRECT, TRANSFERABLE, PARTIAL, and UNKNOWN remain explainable; specialist and scope controls remain intact; missing evidence and ambition are not penalties. A customer can understand what they demonstrated, what appears aligned, why, what is transferable, what CareerOS does not know, and what to do next.

## Privacy And Operations
Tenant isolation, export, deletion, privacy disclosure, safe logging, invite-only controls, private database networking, PITR/backup classification, and bounded-beta observability remain passing.
