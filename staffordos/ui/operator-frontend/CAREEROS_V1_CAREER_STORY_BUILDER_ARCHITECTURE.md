# CareerOS Career Story Builder

The story builder is an additive customer workflow. Employment, consulting, projects, accomplishments, technical builds, certifications, education, speaking or teaching, leadership, volunteer work, and other professional experiences are submitted as separate, tenant-owned CareerSource records.

Every text mode converges on the existing deterministic CareerSource -> CareerFactCandidate -> reviewed CareerFact path. A conversation creates only a bounded text draft; it cannot establish a CareerFact, CareerEvidence, capability, scope, or match authority. Document mode is a visible but disabled contract until secure binary storage, inspection, access control, retention, deletion, and recovery are authorized and proven.

`CURRENT_FACTS_REVIEWED` means the currently submitted source material has been reviewed. `CAREER_STORY_COMPLETE_FOR_NOW` is a reversible customer preference, not a claim of completeness. Adding another source reopens the story without replacing prior authority.
